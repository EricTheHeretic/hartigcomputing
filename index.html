The working part is the gap.

A root stops at its own skin. What feeds the plant is a second body, living in the seams, reaching past the drip line, and lasting only because it gives something back. We copy that, not the organism. Posts carry supply into the middle of a volume. A signal is sent only when what went out comes back whole. Cells, when a laboratory allows them, sit against the mesh. They are not pierced by it. This is not a claim that a forest thinks. A good network feeds its center and pays for what it takes.
